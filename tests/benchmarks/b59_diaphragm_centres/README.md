# B59 - Diaphragm centres of mass and rigidity

Tower A: B58's four unequal columns (one turned 90 degrees), two stories of 3.5 m joined at each level only by
rigid diaphragm D1; masses at the joints only, different in X and Y. Tower C (x = 20 m): a one-element
Shell-Thin slab on four columns in semi-rigid diaphragm SR1, with joint masses.

The table per story and diaphragm: masses, centre of mass, cumulative mass and centre (this story and above),
centre of rigidity (a force on one diaphragm, the other free). A semi-rigid diaphragm: its centre of mass
only.

User wind loads: WCM at D1's centre of mass at each story (the default point); WSF, WSM and WSN on the
semi-rigid floor at given coordinates, into the floor element, spread by mass and through the joint nearest
the centre of mass.

References: hand (statics and the two levels' 6 x 6 stiffness).

Reference files: `hand.json` (56 values).
