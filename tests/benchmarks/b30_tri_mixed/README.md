# B30 - Slab with quads and triangles and a triangular gable wall, on four columns

6 x 6 m Shell-Thin slab (t = 0.2 m) at z = 3.5 m: two quads on the left half, four triangles on the right. A 2
m high triangular gable wall (two Shell-Thin triangles meeting at the ridge joint) stands on the y = 0 edge.
Four 0.4 x 0.4 m columns at the corners, bases fixed.

- DEAD: 8 kN/m^2 down on the slab.
- WIND: 10 kN in X and 5 kN in Y at the ridge, and 1.5 kN/m^2 on the gable in its local 3 (-Y).

Hand: base reaction totals.

Reference files: `hand.json` (5 values).
