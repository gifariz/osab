# B17 Two columns tied at the top by a rigid link (3D)
Fixed columns of different sections (the second rotated 90 degrees) at x = 0 and 5 m, 3.5 m high, their tops
joined by a rigid link, with a 2000 kN/m spring in Y at the slave top. LC1: 30 kN in X at one top, 10 kN in Y
and 80 kN down at the other. LC2: a 15 kN m torque about Z at the master top (the tie turns, so both columns
bend and twist). Hand: each column's closed-form tip stiffness and the rigid-body compatibility of the tops, a
6 x 6 solve.

Reference files: `hand.json` (122 values), `opensees.json` (122 values).
